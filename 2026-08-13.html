Systems & tools

The off-site backup was quietly skipping six folders, and now it is not
The backup that copies this computer off-site had been showing a permissions warning. It turned out not to be cosmetic. Because the permission had never been granted, the backup was unable to read six folders at all — the desktop, documents, downloads, pictures, music and movies — and had been skipping them for at least three months. Nothing announced this: the backup reported itself as finishing normally every hour, with nothing left to send, because the folders it could not open never appeared on its list in the first place. The gap was found by reading the backup's own record of every file it has ever stored and checking which folders never appear in it. The financial records, the payroll history, the balances and the notes were all confirmed present and were never at risk. The permission has now been granted and those six folders will be filled in on their own.
