Systems & tools

Pending card charges are now read straight from the statement
The pending page is parsed automatically and checked against its own stated total, so a layout change fails loudly instead of quietly returning a short list. On today's numbers that check is the difference between reading the card at 83.74% and reading it at 98.84%.
Materials charges now flag at any amount
The old rule only flagged card charges of $2,000.00 or more, so a known supplier billing $1,900.00 was never seen. Materials and unrecognised charges now flag whatever they cost, and each one starts as awaiting a receipt, so one queue answers both what was charged and whether the receipt is filed.
Two checks that disagreed about the same number now have to agree
Two separate calculations of uncashed checks had been $745.00 apart for a day. One was right. They are now compared automatically and a disagreement stops being something someone has to notice.
An independent review of the day's numbers caught a real error
