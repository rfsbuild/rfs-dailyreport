Systems & tools

The automatic backup now pauses during a working session
It commits and pushes every ten minutes, and this morning it published five sets of unreviewed changes while work was still in progress. It can now be paused and resumed on demand, and it restarts itself after twelve hours so a pause that gets forgotten cannot quietly leave the work without a backup.
A check-tracking fault that reported paid checks as unpaid
The bank sometimes records a cleared check under its own reference rather than the number written on it. The tool that lists outstanding checks matched on that number alone, so any check with a mismatch looked permanently unpaid. It now cross-checks the amount as well, and reports anything it cannot match with certainty for a person to confirm rather than deciding on its own.
Check numbers ending in zero were being cut short
A formatting fault trimmed the final digit, so check 3780 displayed as 378. The totals were never wrong, but two different checks could have collided. Fixed and confirmed against the full history.
The QuickBooks export is now kept
Figures taken from QuickBooks used to be transcribed and the export discarded, which left them impossible to re-check later. Today's export is stored with a fingerprint so any number taken from it can be traced back.
